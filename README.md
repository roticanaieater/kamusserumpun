**Kamus Serumpun** is a dictionary-inspired website designed in an interactive way, where users get to choose 2 to 3 Nusantara languages to compare. Some of the languages are divided further into dialects, and language level (like Javanese being mainly divided between <i>ngoko</i> and <i>krama</i>).

As the entry of words here are mostly based on published literary materials rather than interviews with native speakers, each language chosen will have their sources listed in the bibliography above the footer section. Therefore, a report box is available for native speakers to correct the words listed here whenever they spot an error in this website.

---

## ✨ Features

* **Interactive map:** Languages are primarily picked through the map that shows the areas where each language is spoken. Users are able to zoom in or enlarge the map to see small details better.
